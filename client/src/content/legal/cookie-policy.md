2. ## What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, such as your preferences, login status, and settings.

We also use similar technologies like:

* Local Storage and Session Storage: These store data directly in your browser to remember your preferences, Skin Profile, and session information without relying on cookies.  
* Pixels and Web Beacons: These are tiny graphics embedded in our Platform or emails that help us understand how you interact with our content and measure the effectiveness of our communications.  
* Software Development Kits (SDKs): In our mobile/web app, we use SDKs to collect analytics, understand app performance, and personalize your skincare experience.

These tools help us deliver a personalized skincare experience tailored to your skin type, concerns, and routine. They enable us to remember your preferences and provide you with relevant recommendations.

**Cookies vs. Similar Technologies**

For the purposes of this Cookie Policy, references to "cookies" include all of the technologies described above, unless we specify otherwise. The consent and control mechanisms described in this policy apply equally to these similar technologies.

**First-Party vs. Third-Party Cookies**

**First-Party Cookies:** These are set by DermicIQ directly when you visit our Platform. They allow us to remember your preferences, login status, and other information to personalize your experience.

**Third-Party Cookies:** These are set by our directed service providers (such as analytics and infrastructure platforms) to collect information about your interactions with our Platform. These third parties are contractually obligated to use your data only for the specific services they provide to us.

3. ## Cookies We Use

We categorize the cookies and technologies we use as follows:

Essential Cookies \- These are strictly necessary for the Platform to function properly and cannot be disabled.

* Authentication and login sessions  
* Security features (fraud prevention, account protection)  
* Basic navigation and page loading  
* Load balancing and performance optimization  
* UI language preference (en or fr)  
* Cookie consent preference storage  
* DermicIQ first-party operational server and application logs  
* Product search query processing and operational search metrics (server-side / first-party)

Without these cookies and Essential / Strictly Necessary technologies, parts of the site or app (like logging into your account) would not work. We do not require your consent for essential cookies, as the Platform cannot operate without them.

**Essential / Strictly Necessary operational technologies.** Alongside the Essential cookies above, we use **DermicIQ first-party logs** (server and application operational logging used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the Platform) and **product search query processing** for operational search metrics. Search terms are processed for operational search metrics without persistent user identifiers or tracking profiles. These are first-party / server-side operations, not marketing trackers. They are classified as **Essential / Strictly Necessary** — they are **not** Analytics and Performance technologies and are **not** Cookie Functional technologies (Functional remains non-essential and consent-required). No cookie or telemetry Accept/Reject toggle is required for this search and operational-logging path.

Functional Cookies \- These remember your choices to provide a better, personalized experience:

* Remembering your Skin Profile, concerns, and sensitivities  
* Saving your product routine and preferences  
* Display preferences (e.g., dark mode, font size)  
* Session persistence to maintain your login state

These cookies are non-essential and require your explicit consent before they are placed on your device.

Analytics and Performance Cookies \- These help us understand how people use DermicIQ so we can improve it:

* Which pages and features are most popular  
* How users interact with skin quizzes and recommendations  
* Overall usage trends and performance metrics  
* Load times and error reporting  
* Referral sources and traffic patterns

For error, crash, and performance monitoring we use **Sentry**, which remains an Analytics and Performance technology. We do not use Google Analytics. We use analytics and performance data in aggregate form and do not use it to identify you individually. We aggregate or de-identify analytics data where possible. Analytics data is retained for fourteen (14) months; after that period, we delete or anonymize it in accordance with our retention schedule and Privacy Policy. We do not sell this data. Non-essential analytics and performance cookies or SDKs require your explicit consent. **DermicIQ first-party logs** and product search query processing for operational search metrics are Essential / Strictly Necessary (see Essential Cookies above); they are not classified as Analytics and Performance.

Personalization Cookies (No Third-Party Advertising) \- This category includes cookies and SDKs used to personalize your experience on the Platform (e.g., product recommendations, routine tracking) and to measure the effectiveness of our own first-party communications and offers. We do not use these for third-party advertising, retargeting, or ad networks. No data is shared with ad exchanges or demand-side platforms. We do not sell advertising inventory or place third-party advertising cookies on your device:

* Suggested products for your skin type and concerns  
* Progress tracking in your skincare journey  
* Personalized insights and routine adjustments  
* Measuring the effectiveness of our first-party marketing communications  
* Delivering first-party content or offers that may be of interest to you

These cookies are non-essential and require your explicit consent.
